Adaptation for Battuta by Wormforce, 2026: offline STEP tessellation with
occt-import-js, conversion to GLB, right-handed Y-up coordinates, recentering,
semantic mesh names, and modified display materials. The webpage applies a lime
stem and translucent housing treatment. This is not official Cherry imagery,
endorsement, or a representation of a specific commercial switch's materials.

The upstream repository additionally declares an MIT license; its notice is
included in LICENSE-MIT.txt. Source-file metadata specifies CC BY-SA 4.0, so this
adaptation retains that license. These terms cover mx-switch.glb, not unrelated
Battuta site code or other artwork. Attribution and license must accompany any
redistribution of this adapted model.

The photographic-style hero and product illustrations are separate generated
visual assets and are not derived from this CAD model.
